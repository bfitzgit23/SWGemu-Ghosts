# Ghosts Patch Notes - 25 November 2026

## Holocrons
- Holocrons now have a working **Recharge Force** radial option (right-click the
  holocron). Using it consumes the holocron and restores your entire Force bar.
- The refill is limited to once per hour per character.
- A holocron you can no longer study - because your stage is complete, or you are
  already a full Master - is now consumed for the same refill instead of being
  refused with no effect.
- If your Force is already full, or the hour has not passed, the holocron is kept.

## Force Lightning
- Force Lightning and Electric Judgment damage now scale with your Jedi path
  instead of sitting on the flat datatable values. Light and Dark share one
  multiplier; Grey Jedi, who receive no FRS council bonus, get a slightly higher
  one.
- After testing, the multiplier was halved, so lightning is stronger than before
  but no longer competing with a lightsaber hit for hit.
- **Force Lightning 2** (both single target and cone) now sets the target on fire,
  applying a burning damage-over-time effect, like the flamethrower attacks.
- Other Force powers (choke, push, and the rest) are unchanged.

## Mines
- Minefield detonations now hit for roughly twelve times their old damage, which
  puts a placed mine in the same range as a lightsaber hit instead of a few
  hundred points.

## Armour and Clothing Attachments
- Every armour and clothing attachment now rolls its skill modifier at the +25
  cap, whatever level the creature that dropped it was.
- This fixes the Nightsister stronghold, faction, and other low-level drops that
  were arriving as +6, +10 or +12 at best.
